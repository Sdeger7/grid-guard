01

What we built

It started as a scripted 30-level tower defense (a full vertical slice), then evolved — on your direction — into a realistic single-map energy-management sim: production ↔ storage ↔ consumption ↔ money ↔ threat ↔ security, all interlocked.

  1. Architecture & foundation

    Isometric 2:1 projection + depth sorting, tile-space paths, data models, offline save (shared_preferences), Riverpod, and a mock MonetizationService — no real ad/IAP SDK, but wired for one.

  2. Core gameplay

    PV economy, Scissor Barrier (area slow) + Shock Transformer (chaining damage), wave spawner, drone/malware enemies, win/lose, 3-star scoring.

  3. UI, feel & store

    Full HUD, level select, win/lose screens, store mockup, screen shake, geometric arc/burst VFX, audio hooks, interstitial pacing.

  4. The pivot → energy sim

    Real energy flow (PV→BESS→Data Center), money from data (not kills), day/night cycle, wind power, endless BASE·SURVIVAL mode with escalating raids, DC workloads, and security gating.

Data Center workloads — the risk/reward dial

Pick what the Data Center processes. Higher-value data pays more, draws more power, and — realistically — attracts more & harder raids (threat ×). You can't take a job without enough security (🛡) built first.

Threat × shortens the gap between raids and scales how many enemies each raid brings. Security = Shock Transformer (×2) + Scissor Barrier (×1), each scaled by tier.

07

Resources & systems

Four resources

⚡ Energy
Operational fuel, stored in the BESS. From PV (sun) + wind. Spent by the Data Center and tower shots.
💰 Money
Build & upgrade currency. Earned by the Data Center while powered.
🎯 Score
Performance metric. From enemy kills only.
🛡 Security
Defensive strength. Gates which workloads you can run.

Live systems

Day / Night
80s cycle. PV only produces in daylight (peak at noon); the board darkens at night.
Wind
Oscillates 15%–100% over time — independent of the sun, so it can cover nights.
Threat
Workload threat scales raid frequency & size.
Security gate
High-value jobs stay locked until defenses are built.
08

Modes

🏭

Base · Survival

The single endless map. Every free tile is buildable; raids escalate on a timer (boss every 5th). No win — grow until the core falls.

🗺️

Campaign

30 scripted levels across 5 zones (6 each), difficulty ramping, an Overload Boss Wave capping every zone.

Market

Store mockup — cosmetic skins & a Starter Pack tile, wired to the mock monetization service (no real purchases).
