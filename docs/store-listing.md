# Grid Guard — store listing and positioning

Everything here is written to be pasted into a store listing, a press email or
a pitch deck. The English is the primary; the Turkish follows.

---

## Positioning, in one line

**A working simulation of the energy business — generation, storage, data
centres, mining and the power market — that happens to have drones attacking it
at night.**

That order matters. The simulation is the product. The raids are what make it a
game rather than a spreadsheet, and they are the only invented part of it.

---

## What is actually real

This is the whole marketing argument, and it is unusual enough to lead with.

- **The sun.** Solar output is computed from the site's real latitude and
  longitude on today's real date, using standard solar geometry: declination,
  hour angle, the equation of time and air mass. A panel in Konya in June works
  from before six in the morning until after eight at night. The same panel in
  Erzurum in December gets nine hours of weak, low-angle light. Nobody tuned
  those numbers — they fall out of the astronomy.
- **The weather.** Cloud cover, wind speed and temperature come from live
  observations for the site's actual coordinates. A storm over your province is
  a storm over your panels.
- **The places.** Twenty-one real locations across Türkiye, Europe, the
  Americas, Central Asia and East Asia, carrying their published solar and wind
  resource. Aberdeen is wind. Seville is sun. Amarillo is both, and the land
  costs nothing. Hamburg is grey and pays the most.
- **The market.** Power prices follow the shape a solar-heavy grid actually
  takes: cheap at midday when everyone's solar floods the network, expensive at
  dusk and overnight when nothing is generating. That is the duck curve, and it
  is why storage is the centre of this game rather than an accessory.
- **The contracts.** Fixed-term deals at a locked price, and a sell contract is
  a real commitment: fail to deliver and the grid buys the shortfall on your
  behalf at a punitive rate. That is an imbalance charge, and it is how energy
  trading works.
- **The mining.** A capped currency at 2,500,000 units, with difficulty rising
  as the reserve empties, so the total approaches the cap and can never cross
  it. Early operators mine at nearly full speed; later ones work for a fraction
  of it.

**What is invented:** the drones.

## Long description

**You run an independent power site in a zone the utility gave up on.**

Panels only make power when the sun is actually up over your province — real
latitude, real date, real weather. Turbines run when the wind is really blowing.
Everything you generate goes into batteries, and everything on site draws from
them: the data centres earning your money, every shot your defences fire, the
intelligence centre that tells you when the next raid is coming.

**Every machine books its own contract.** Web hosting is safe and pays little.
AI compute pays well and eats power. Bank records and government traffic pay
properly and require a security floor you have to build before anyone will
sign. Crypto mining pays no cash at all — it mints ⚡WATT, the only currency in
the game that buys permanent upgrades, and there will only ever be 2,500,000 of
it.

**Power has a price, and it moves.** Cheap at midday when the whole region is
dumping solar, dear after dark when nothing is generating. Sign a four-hour buy
contract at noon and carry that price through the evening peak. Sign a sell
contract and you have to actually deliver — whatever you cannot supply is bought
on your behalf at 1.6× spot. A large battery is not a safety net here. It is the
business.

**And every night, something comes for it.** Raiders fly in from every edge and
go for what hurts: the data centre running the hottest contract first, then your
intelligence, then your batteries. Be there when a window opens and you fight it
with overcharge, emergency shutdown and a blackout that leaves them with nothing
to lock onto. Miss it and your automated defences meet it alone, and you find
out in the morning what it cost.

The site is permanent. It keeps running while the app is closed. Nothing you
build is ever lost to a defeat screen — losing the core is a blackout you
recover from, not a game over.

**Features**

- Real solar geometry and live weather for 21 real locations worldwide
- Buy or rent land anywhere; hold several sites and wheel power between them,
  paying for the distance
- A power market with fixed-term contracts, locked prices and imbalance charges
- Five data centre workloads with security requirements and real trade-offs
- ⚡WATT: a capped, unbuyable currency with rising mining difficulty
- Weekly tournaments where everyone gets the same seed — the only thing that
  differs is the decisions
- Fully playable offline

## Press angle

For energy and technology press, the hook is not "new tower defence". It is:

> **Somebody built a mobile game that models the duck curve, imbalance charges
> and mining difficulty adjustment — and it is fun.**

The audience that will carry this is people who work in energy. They will
recognise what the game is doing within a minute of opening it, and that
recognition is the thing worth sharing. Lead every technical conversation with
the solar geometry and the market curve; lead every player-facing conversation
with the raids.

## Türkçe

### Tek cümlelik konum

**Elektrik üretimi, depolama, veri merkezleri, madencilik ve enerji piyasasının
çalışan bir simülasyonu — üstüne gece gelen dronlar.**

Sıralama önemli: simülasyon ürünün kendisi, akınlar ise onu tablodan oyuna
çeviren şey. Ve uydurulmuş tek parça onlar.

### Gerçek olan ne

- **Güneş.** Sahanın gerçek enlem ve boylamında, bugünün tarihinde, standart
  güneş geometrisiyle hesaplanıyor. Konya'da haziranda panel altıdan önce
  çalışmaya başlar; aynı panel Erzurum'da aralıkta dokuz saat zayıf, alçak açılı
  ışık alır. Bu sayıları kimse ayarlamadı — astronomiden çıkıyor.
- **Hava durumu.** Bulut, rüzgâr ve sıcaklık, sahanın gerçek koordinatları için
  canlı ölçümlerden geliyor.
- **Yerler.** Türkiye, Avrupa, Amerika, Orta Asya ve Doğu Asya'da 21 gerçek
  konum, yayınlanmış güneş ve rüzgâr potansiyelleriyle.
- **Piyasa.** Öğlen ucuz, akşam ve gece pahalı — yani gerçek duck curve. Bu
  yüzden depolama oyunun aksesuarı değil, merkezi.
- **Kontratlar.** Sabit süreli, fiyatı kilitli. Satış kontratında teslim
  edemediğin kısım senin adına 1.6 kat fiyatla alınır — gerçek dengeleme cezası.
- **Madencilik.** 2.500.000 birimle sınırlı bir para birimi; rezerv boşaldıkça
  zorluk artıyor, yani tavan matematiksel olarak aşılamıyor.

**Uydurma olan:** dronlar.

### Basın açısı

Enerji ve teknoloji basını için kanca "yeni bir tower defense" değil:

> **Duck curve'ü, dengesizlik cezasını ve madencilik zorluk ayarını modelleyen
> bir mobil oyun yapmışlar — üstelik eğlenceli.**

Bu oyunu taşıyacak kitle enerji sektöründe çalışan insanlar. Oyunu açtıktan bir
dakika sonra ne yapıldığını anlarlar, ve paylaşmaya değer bulacakları şey tam
olarak o tanıma anıdır.
